# Tirak Product Positioning

## Category

**Primary category:** Local guided travel experience marketplace.

Tirak helps travelers choose a concrete activity in Thailand, understand exactly what it includes, request a date, and pay for the confirmed real-world service.

## Positioning Statement

For travelers who want a clear and locally informed way to explore Thailand, Tirak is a guided-experience marketplace that combines structured itineraries with verified local expertise. Unlike generic activity directories, Tirak makes the itinerary, service scope, guide credentials, booking status, and total price explicit from discovery through completion.

## Value Proposition

### For Travelers

- Browse by activity and destination rather than by a person's availability.
- Know the route, duration, inclusions, meeting point, and total price before requesting.
- See the responsible guide's languages, credentials, and service reviews.
- Pay Tirak only after the requested itinerary is confirmed.
- Coordinate practical details in a booking-scoped channel.

### For Local Guides

- Publish clearly scoped cultural and travel services.
- Manage availability at the experience-booking level.
- Build a professional reputation from delivered itineraries and service reviews.
- Receive booking records with explicit scope, date, group size, and traveler needs.

## Differentiation

Tirak competes on clarity, local depth, accountable delivery, and a focused Thailand inventory. It does not compete on personality matching, romantic or social connection, spontaneous one-to-one company, or access to people.

## Proof Hierarchy

1. Experience title and outcome
2. Itinerary and route
3. Inclusions, exclusions, duration, and group size
4. Guide qualifications and languages
5. Service reviews tied to completed bookings
6. Server-authoritative total and payment receipt
7. Safety, reporting, support, and cancellation controls

## Required Associations

- Guided travel
- Cultural learning
- Local expertise
- Clear itinerary
- Verified guide
- Secure marketplace booking
- Practical trip coordination

## Associations to Avoid

- Dating, romance, or matchmaking
- Companionship or paid company
- Escorts, adult services, or discreet encounters
- Friendship as a purchased outcome
- “Vibe” or chemistry matching
- Hourly-person rates
- General chat or “available tonight” discovery
- Direct cash payment to a person

## App Store Category Fit

Tirak belongs in **Travel** because its transactions are for real-world guided travel services. Checkout is attached to a confirmed itinerary, not a digital good and not a social relationship.
