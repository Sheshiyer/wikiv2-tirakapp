# Glossary of Terms

## Core Entities

### Local Guide (Supply)
A Thai local, ID-checked by the Tirak team, who shares cultural experiences in Thailand. Not a scripted "tour guide"—a local guide offers authentic, personal connection and local knowledge. They set their own rates and schedule.

### Traveler (Demand)
A visitor to Thailand (often a digital nomad or solo traveler) seeking authentic connection and local insight. They value "vibe" over scripted history lessons.

## Business Model

### Commission
The fee Tirak takes from each booking.
- **Founding Guides**: 0% commission until 7 March 2027.
- **Planned standard commission**: 15-20% (significantly lower than industry standard of 30-40%).
- **Soft launch payments**: in-app payments are off; travelers pay the guide in cash at the meeting point.

### Supply-First Strategy
Our marketing philosophy. We believe **Supply Creates Demand**. By building a high-quality, diverse pool of local guides, travelers will naturally be attracted to the platform. 80% of resources go to supply acquisition and retention.

## Features

### Vibe-Based Matching
The core value prop for travelers. Instead of filtering by "activity" (e.g., Temple Tour), travelers filter by "vibe" (e.g., Foodie, Night Owl, Artsy, Chill). Matches are based on personality fit.

### Instant Toggle
A feature for local guides allowing them to turn availability on/off instantly. Supports the "gig economy" nature of the work—host when you want, stop when you're busy.

### Verified Profile
A local guide profile whose government ID has been checked by the Tirak team before approval.

## Marketing Terms

### DTC (Direct-to-Consumer)
Our launch strategy. We market directly to local guides and travelers via social media (FB/IG/TikTok) rather than going through travel agencies or aggregators.

### Bootstrapped
Our financial status. We are self-funded with a lean budget, necessitating high-impact, low-cost marketing strategies (like this wiki and organic social content).
