> Internal planning document. It lives outside the Astro content collection and is **not published** on the wiki site.

## Soft launch at a glance

**Date:** Monday 21 December 2026
**City:** Bangkok
**Positioning:** Local guides for cultural experiences in Thailand

### In scope

- Android app
- Web app (PWA) for iPhone
- Cash paid by the traveler to the guide at the meeting point
- Invite-led: travelers join in small invite waves

### Not in scope

- In-app payments
- SMS login
- iOS App Store release
- Other cities
- Paid ads
- Press

## Timeline

| Dates | Workstream | Goal |
|-------|-----------|------|
| 24 Nov – 4 Dec | Fix blockers | All launch blockers closed; data and legal items done by **27 Nov** |
| 24 Nov – 18 Dec | Recruit founding guides | **12 approved** guides, **8 with complete profiles** |
| 7 – 16 Dec | Friends-only trial | 10–20 travelers; goal of **5 completed bookings** |
| Thu 17 Dec | Go/no-go | Decide on launch |
| 17 – 20 Dec | Fix window | Fix what the trial and go/no-go surfaced |
| **Mon 21 Dec** | **Soft launch** | Smaller invite waves (~25 travelers/week) through 4 Jan, then ~50/week |
| Mon 15 Feb 2027 | Checkpoint | Review results against targets |

**Fallback:** if the go/no-go on Thu 17 Dec fails, the soft launch moves to **Monday 11 January 2027**.

## Decisions made

- The vendor directory has been removed from the wiki and purged from the repository.
- Positioning is **"local guides"** for cultural experiences in Thailand (not "companions" or "hangouts").
- Founding guides pay **0% commission until 7 March 2027**.
- Launch in late December (Mon 21 Dec 2026).

## Targets by 14 February 2027

| Metric | Target |
|--------|--------|
| Guides with complete profiles | 25 |
| Guides active weekly | 15 |
| New travelers per week | 40 |
| Booking requests | 80 |
| Requests accepted within 2 hours | 60% |
| Completed bookings with a review | 30 |
| Safety reports open more than 24 hours | 0 |

## Risks

- **Shorter trial** — the friends-only trial is short, so it gives less evidence before launch.
- **Holiday peak** — guides may be away, and founder support load is higher over the holidays.
- **Android build** — there is no slack before 4 Dec.
- **New Year's Eve crowds** — use public meeting points only.
- **One short fix window** — only 17–20 Dec between go/no-go and launch.

- **Monday 10:00:** weekly plan
- **Friday 16:00:** weekly review
